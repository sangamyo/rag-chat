    How it works

    1. Chunk: text is split into ~900-character passages along paragraph and sentence boundaries.
    2. Embed: each passage becomes a 384-d vector with bge-small-en-v1.5.
    3. Retrieve: your question is matched by meaning (cosine) and keywords (BM25), merged with reciprocal rank fusion.
    4. Generate: an LLM running on your GPU answers only from those passages and cites them.

    Your files never leave this device.
